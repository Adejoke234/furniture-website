About Oak & Ember

Oak & Ember is a family furniture store in Lagos. We choose every piece for comfort, quality and style, so you can create a home you love spending time in.

From compact chairs for small apartments to full living-room sets, our team helps you pick pieces that fit your space and budget. We deliver, assemble and take away the packaging, so all you need to do is relax.
