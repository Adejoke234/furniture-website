# Oak & Ember Furniture (Demo)

A responsive website for **Oak & Ember**, a fictional furniture store in Lagos, built as a frontend portfolio project.

**Live site:** https://adejoke234.github.io/furniture-website/

> Portfolio demo: the brand, prices, contact details and reviews are samples.

## Features
- Responsive layout for phones, tablets and desktops
- Product search that filters the product cards
- **Order Now** buttons that fill the chosen product into the contact form
- "Read More" toggle in the About section
- Contact form with validation and success/error messages (sent through Formspree)
- Accessible labels, image descriptions and keyboard focus styles

## Built with
HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript

## Connecting the contact form (Formspree)
1. Create a free account at https://formspree.io and click **New Form**.
2. Copy your form ID (the part after `/f/` in the form's endpoint, e.g. `xyzabcd`).
3. In `index.html`, replace `YOUR_FORM_ID` in
   `action="https://formspree.io/f/YOUR_FORM_ID"` with your ID.
4. Push the change. Messages from the form will arrive in your email.
